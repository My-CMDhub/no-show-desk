NO-SHOW DESK.

Know who won't turn up, before they don't.

Every morning at 8, No-Show Desk ranks your next three days of arrivals by how likely each guest is to cancel, learned from your own past bookings. It drafts a message to the few worth asking, checks every date and price, and sends nothing until you say so.
