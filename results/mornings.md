# Thirty mornings at a 20-room motel (2017)

Each morning: 20 upcoming no-deposit bookings, TabPFN learns from 3,000 earlier bookings, the 5 riskiest are flagged.

- Flagged guests who really cancelled: **84 of 150** (56%)
- Five guests picked at random would have caught about **43** (29%)
- Cancellations caught by the flags: **84 of 172** (49%), while messaging a quarter of guests
- Mornings where the flags caught more than chance: 29 of 30
